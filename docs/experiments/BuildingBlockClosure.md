## Limits and reopening conditions

- Restricted mixed menus and atomic-carrier provenance receive the final
  explicit controls above; their hypotheses must remain visible afterward.
- Conditional consistency for a new law preference requires a named staged
  consumer and a substantive composition hypothesis. Existing expectation and
  possibility results already have both supporting and refuting controls.
- Infinite stochastic paths require an actual path-law consumer. The static
  finite and PMF APIs do not make a path-existence claim.
- The permanent shared-mathematics package owner is a deployment/maintenance
  decision. MEC-009 already compiles the canonical GameTheory mathematical
  theorem through the actual MathEconLib demand path; another copy or arbitrary
  package skeleton would not choose the maintainer or release boundary.
- Debreu/vNM breadth, further equilibrium existence, algorithms, and complete
  boundary-price classifications are theorem or domain development until they
  expose duplicated semantics, excessive transport, or a missing operation.
- IC classes versus ordinary predicates and alternative record packaging remain
  replaceable. Existing consumers currently provide no new result that would
  discriminate those representations. The working guarded-core alternative is
  retained as evidence rather than rejected by proof length.

Exhausting this queue is an empirical stopping point for the identified
building-block competitions, not a completeness theorem about economics.
